# 293 Free Use License

Version 1.0

A license for code, games, apps, art, audio and anything else you make. Anyone can use, modify and share the work for any purpose. Nobody can sell it, including the author.

## Allowed

- Use, copy, modify and redistribute, with or without credit
- Commercial use, as long as nothing is charged for the work
- Donations, as long as donors get nothing extra
- Monetized videos, streams and reviews about the work
- Including the work in a bigger paid product, under the rules in Section 4
- Selling physical goods, including physical copies of the work

## Not allowed

- Selling digital copies of the work, a modified version, or paid features of it
- Ads, sponsorships, referral fees or selling user data from the work
- Charging for setup or support
- Using the work as background music, footage or art in unrelated monetized content
- AI training, unless the maintainer publishes a notice allowing it

## Required

- Keep the license text, plus the "Applies to" and "Source" lines
- Publish the full source of any modified version, free
- Give modified versions a different name

## How to use it

1. Copy `LICENSE` into the root of your project.
2. Fill in the placeholders: year, your name, project name and source URL.
3. To allow AI training, publish an AI notice with the project saying what is allowed.
